How they connect

One profile.
Everything downstream.

The three features aren't three separate products — they're three views of the same profile. Update your stats once; everything else re-computes.

i.

You update your profile

New tournament round, new SAT score, new swing video. One place.

ii.

Fit scores re-rank

Every one of 1,100+ programs re-scores overnight. Your shortlist updates.

iii.

Playbook rewrites itself

New moves surface based on what changed. Old ones drop off. No stale checklists.

Notes for review · not shipping copy

What this mockup is (and isn't).

  • Is: concept art in Drip's real design tokens. Three feature sections you can click through and evaluate as a story.
  • Isn't: a working product. No live data, no login, no fetches. Every number is illustrative.
  • Aesthetic call: we borrowed Nexus's Fit Score math (Athletic + Academic → 0–100, tiered Scholarship / Target / Reach) but rejected their dashboard aesthetic. Ours reads like a magazine callout, not a Bloomberg terminal — that's your differentiator.
  • Playbook voice: clean checklist per your call, not the editorial "weekly letter" alternative. Kept the warm palette so it still feels like Drip.
  • Profile framing: deliberately positioned as "one source of truth" for the whole product. Fits DRY, fits the Zen of Python's "one obvious way," and gives us a real story about why the three features belong together.
  • Next pass if approved: extract shared CSS tokens into styles.css, wire real data into the profile card the same way the live page hydrates Luke's info, and decide whether Fit Score lives inside the paid tier or as a free lead-magnet.
  • What we did NOT do: touch index.html, edit netlify.toml, add a build step, or push anything. Standalone file. Zero risk to prod.
